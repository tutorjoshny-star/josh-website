About

I grew up on Long Island and graduated as salutatorian from Half Hollow Hills High School before studying engineering at the University of Pennsylvania, where I earned both my bachelor's and master's degrees and graduated summa cum laude. After scoring in the 99th percentile on both the SAT and ACT myself, I eventually found my way back to standardized testing as a tutor, teaching SAT and math prep both in the classroom and one-on-one.

My engineering background shapes the way I teach. I like to break complicated problems into manageable pieces, understand why something works, and find a different way to explain it when the first approach doesn't click. Rather than relying on memorization or one-size-fits-all strategies, I help students develop clear ways of thinking through problems that they can apply even when a question looks unfamiliar.

What I enjoy most about tutoring is seeing a student go from feeling stuck to realizing that a problem is something they can actually handle. My goal is for students to leave our sessions not only better prepared for the next test, but more confident in their ability to work through challenging material on their own.
