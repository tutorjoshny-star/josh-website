About

I grew up on Long Island and attended Half Hollow Hills High School, where I graduated as salutatorian before going on to study engineering at the University of Pennsylvania. At Penn, I completed both my bachelor's and master's degrees in four years and graduated summa cum laude. Having scored in the 99th percentile on both the SAT and ACT myself, I eventually found my way back to standardized testing as a tutor, teaching SAT and math prep at a New York test-prep company. There, I taught classes of up to 15 students while also working individually with students who needed more personalized support. My engineering background has a big influence on the way I teach. I like to break complicated problems into pieces, understand why something works, and find another way to explain it when the first approach doesn't click. I bring that same mindset to tutoring. Rather than emphasizing memorization or one-size-fits-all strategies, I help students develop clear ways of thinking through problems that they can apply to unfamiliar questions.The part of tutoring I find most rewarding is figuring out exactly where a student is getting stuck and finding the explanation or approach that finally makes something click. I want students to see measurable improvement, of course, but I also want them to leave our sessions feeling more confident in their own ability to work through challenging material.
